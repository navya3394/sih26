# AyuSetu terminology provenance

This release uses a provenance-first terminology model.

## Authoritative sources

1. NAMASTE Portal, Ministry of Ayush: https://namaste.ayush.gov.in/sat_Ayurveda
2. WHO International Standard Terminologies on Ayurveda: https://www.who.int/publications/b/55789
3. WHO ICD-11 API: https://icd.who.int/icdapi
4. Ministry of Ayush / DGHS Standard Treatment Guidelines on Musculoskeletal Disorders (April 2024): https://ayush.gov.in/resources/pdf/publichealth/management-of-common-musculoskeletal-disorders-in-ayurveda-system-of-medicine.pdf
5. Ministry of Ayush Standard Treatment Guidelines on Metabolic Disorders: https://ayush.gov.in/resources/pdf/publichealth/STG_Ayuveda_Metabolc_Disorders.pdf

These mappings are marked `verified` because the cited Government of India publications explicitly print the NAMASTE morbidity code together with the ICD-11 TM2 and biomedical ICD-11 code.

The remaining terminology records are retained for search coverage but are marked `unmapped` until an authoritative crosswalk or expert-reviewed mapping is available. No synthetic TM2/MMS mapping is claimed for them.
